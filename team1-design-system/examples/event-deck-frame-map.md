# Example: six-slide local meetup frame map

This is a method example, not approved event copy. Bracketed facts must be supplied and verified.

| Output | Narrative job | Bundled template frame | Suggested authentic assets | Guardrail |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome the room | Slide 1 — cover / bumper | Primary Team1 wordmark; dark unframed background | Add only verified city/date/partner facts |
| 2 | Explain Team1 | Slide 2 — foundation + three proof points | Community-mosaic Team1 graphic | Use current sourced metrics or non-numeric verbs such as Learn / Meet / Build |
| 3 | Show tonight’s journey | Slide 3 — three-stage progression | Team1 mosaic, one real event photo, Team1 wordmark | Keep arrows only if the sequence is real |
| 4 | Make the community tangible | Slide 9 — photo mosaic | Source documentary photography or organizer-approved local photos | Do not imply global source photos were taken locally |
| 5 | Offer ways to participate | Slide 10 — six-module editorial grid | No extra decorative asset required | Keep each module short and practical |
| 6 | Close with one action | Slide 13 — CTA + cinematic hero | Cinematic Team1 hero | Use one approved human-readable destination; fresh tested QR only if needed |

Before delivery, verify fonts and glyphs in the target language, remove stale links, renumber pages, inspect all slides at full size, and export a PDF fallback.
