# Merchandise campaign composition

Status: **derived, medium-specific guidance**, added 2026-09-08 from owner-approved ETHRome and Blockchain Beach designs. This module guides new sticker and wearable concepts. It does not replace global identity, change approved logos, or authorize another event's palette, partner relationship, or artwork reuse. See [comparison evidence](../examples/merch-campaign-comparison.md).

## Start from the actual approved final

When the owner supplies a final SVG, AI or PDF, that file supersedes earlier generated previews and intermediate percentage adjustments for that deliverable. Inspect its actual paths, clipping, proportions, palette and rendered export. Preserve it byte-for-byte for an unchanged handoff; retain earlier versions separately. Do not silently normalize approved art back to an earlier proposal.

A screenshot or outlined file can establish visual weight without establishing a font family. Record a font as unknown when no reliable metadata/source exists; do not claim a similar typeface is the exact original. Separate observed design decisions from recommendations for future work.

## Choose the dominant element

**Typography-led sticker:** use a compact, heavy slogan, tight but readable line spacing and deliberate line breaks. A stepped or staggered arrangement may carry the composition. The event and host marks support the slogan in distinct areas with clear space. Avoid turning short, collectible copy into a miniature event flyer or generic rounded UI card.

**Illustration-led wearable:** use one recognizable local motif and a smaller compact slogan. Let illustration width and visual mass establish the balance, with generous material showing around the complete print. Blockchain Beach's final wave group is approximately 2.5 times its slogan width; this is a campaign observation, not a universal ratio. Adapt front, back, tote and sticker separately.

An instruction such as “text 50% smaller” or “waves 75% bigger” applies to the referenced version and specified element. Name the baseline and whether the percentage means width/height or area. Never propagate it mechanically to another event or continue applying it after a newer final is approved. Do not claim an exact scaling operation when only visual emphasis changed.

## Lettering and copy

Use short, locally meaningful builder humor: a Roman phrase or historical contrast for Rome; sand, sea and shipping code for a beach hackathon. Keep one dominant message. Dates, venues, prize amounts and administrative copy belong on event collateral unless specifically requested on the collectible.

Global headings remain Kanit Medium 500 and body Kanit Light 300. For an explicitly approved heavy merchandise campaign, Kanit Bold outlines are an available **campaign treatment**, as used in the Blockchain Beach production reconstruction. Aeonik remains a controlled alternative with verified access and licensing. Neither treatment changes global tokens or allows rebuilding a logo from type. If reconstructing a generated mockup, disclose the font substitution and review the actual vector proof.

## Illustration and palette

Use recognizable place-specific silhouettes, simplified negative space and a limited flat ink palette. The ETHRome final uses red/white Roman architecture and a wolf with a laptop; the illustrative forms are paths, not embedded generated bitmaps. Prefer clean scalable construction over gradients, texture, shading and tiny details that will disappear at sticker size. A useful set can mix an illustration, a slogan, a brand strip and a standalone mark; it need not repeat one card layout.

Team1 primary red remains #E6212F. Solid red fields are suitable for an approved typography-led merchandise campaign, with high-contrast white lettering. This is not a default for all Team1 media. Campaign accents may evoke the location when explicitly approved and recorded: Blockchain Beach used sand #EBCB8B and sea blue #058AFF on black, ochre #966019 and blue #3055B3 on natural/white material. These accents are not new global brand colors. Select contrast for the actual substrate, and validate ink matching with the supplier.

## Marks and sticker edges

Use authentic event logos when the brief asks for the event identity, rather than substituting plain typeset event names. Keep Team1, Avalanche and event marks as separate authentic assets with traceable provenance, original proportions and the correct light/dark variant. Do not infer a new permanent lockup from one campaign strip.

Do not generalize a cropped partner mark, traced outline, recoloring or crowded placement seen in an approved composition into a global permission. Core no-alteration and clear-space rules still govern new work; an unusual approved final is a scoped precedent that requires its own owner context.

Circles, rectangles, strips and silhouette cuts may coexist. A white sticker border belongs to the substrate/cut design, not an effect added to an official logo. Keep printable artwork and the supplier's cut path separate. A visible black or magenta border is not proof of a production-ready cutting contour.

## Handoff and verification

- Keep the approved source, production master and mockup clearly labeled. A photo mockup is a visualization, not print artwork or a physical proof.
- Deliver scalable paths with outlined or appropriately embedded type and self-contained assets. An SVG containing raster illustrations is mixed-media; do not call it fully vector. Inspect for linked images, missing fonts and unsupported clipping.
- Render the actual master on its intended light/dark substrate and compare it with the approved final. Where a renderer mishandles SVG clipping or masks, verify with a second capable renderer before changing the art.
- Give separate artwork and cut-guide files, proposed physical dimensions and a placement proof when preparing production. Do not print the cut guide or assume an RGB PDF is CMYK/PDF-X.
- Export high-resolution previews from native artwork where possible. Increasing a PNG's dimensions does not add detail to any embedded bitmap.
- Confirm supplier dimensions, material, bleed, minimum strokes, ink/underbase, output profile and physical or photographed proof. Design approval does not establish those manufacturing facts or authorize an order.
- Verify the actual recipient, attachment and delivery status after an authorized handoff. If a messaging application rejects SVG, preserve the original in a ZIP or provide a verified PDF; never report a failed upload as sent.
