# Photography and video

## Photography direction

Show real participation: a venue establishing view, speakers in context, engaged audiences, hands-on activity, networking/candid moments, signage or QR detail, and a group portrait with a recognizable current Team1 element. Prefer populated, meaningful scenes; remove empty-chair distractions and avoid extreme closeups that lose context.

Keep skin tones natural, protect faces and key actions in crops, and do not over-filter everything red. Avoid generic stock, synthetic crowds, staged crypto clichés, and images presented as documentary evidence when they are not.

## Required event delivery

1. Produce a first selection of **25** usable images.
2. Choose the strongest **5** for priority delivery.
3. Deliver each of those five in both **16:9** and **4:5**, for **10 final crops**.
4. Deliver through the approved operational channels with stable filenames and a rights/provenance record.

For group photos, capture a Team1-led banner/flag version and, when relevant, an approved partner/sponsor variant. Team1 remains visually primary; partner marks receive optical parity without taking over the host identity.

## Rights record

Before external use, record photographer/source, consent or release status, permitted channels, territory, expiry, minor/people flags, venue rights, and third-party marks. Treat attendee faces, voices, raw footage, meeting recordings, music, and event media as restricted by default. Do not package them merely because they are accessible in an internal source.

If permission, context, or privacy cannot be established, use a deliberate non-photo composition built from current identity, type, color, and event information. Never expose names, badges, attendee lists, contact details, applications, or private screens. Do not imply that people shown at a prior event will attend a new one.

## Video profile

Video leans on Aeonik for legibility, with Kanit for headlines. Use sentence case and minimal on-screen text. The formal caption recipe at 1920 × 1080 is:

- Aeonik Medium, size 57;
- centered white text;
- black shadow at 100% opacity, angle 135°, distance 3, blur/size 6, spread 12;
- bottom-center placement at X 0, Y -106.

Scale proportionally only when the output size changes and inspect on the actual viewing device. This video rule does not change global typography.

Include an opener, lower thirds, caption-safe area, subtitles/captions, end card, and a music/license field. Verify text timing, spelling, speaker consent, partner marks, loudness, caption accuracy, mobile legibility, and a no-audio comprehension path before delivery.
