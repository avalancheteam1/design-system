# Events, print, signage, and merchandise

## Event workflow

The July 2026 event process is the workflow authority. Submit the event proposal at least 15 days in advance; three weeks is preferred. After approval, request the official flyer and the required X/Luma assets. Keep the event page private until content and visual quality control are complete. Upload the report and approved media within 48 hours after the event.

The source contains conflicting design-delivery estimates. Do not promise a design SLA until the process owner resolves it. Event names, dates, venues, partners, and destinations require owner approval.

## Event kit

A complete brief should define the event owner, audience, locale/timezone, approved name, format, venue, partners, required channels, print dimensions, registration/onboarding destination, photography plan, accessibility, and post-event deliverables. Keep Luma, social, slides, signage, and print visually related without forcing one composition into every ratio.

Recompose each requested format for its real crop, reading distance, and production constraints. Common digital sizes are 1080 × 1350 feed, 1080 × 1920 story, and 1200 × 630 social preview. A4, roll-up, badge/ticket, and other print sizes must come from the printer or hardware template; no universal bleed or hidden-base dimension is authoritative. Keep essential content inside platform/vendor safe zones and inspect full-size plus thumbnail/distance views.

Main banners and flags stay Team1-led. Partner/sponsor marks use approximately equal perceived size and balanced spacing. Photograph one Team1-led group setup and one approved partner/sponsor variant when applicable.

## QR safety

- Generate a fresh QR only from an owner-approved destination; never reuse an archived QR or extract one from a screenshot.
- Prefer the current approved Team1/country route or tracking path supplied by the campaign owner; never infer a regional URL from an old artifact.
- Record destination, owner, purpose, creation date, review date, and expiry/revocation plan.
- Include a visible short destination or fallback action. A QR must never be the only access route.
- Protect a clear quiet zone, use strong contrast, and keep it away from folds, seams, glare, and busy imagery.
- Test the final export, a physical proof, the intended distance, multiple devices, and the post-deployment destination.

## Print and signage preflight

Confirm final size, orientation, substrate, bleed, trim, safe area, finishing, viewing distance, color/export profile, resolution, and vendor requirements before design. Prefer approved vector logos. Record whether fonts must be embedded or outlined, then preflight the final PDF. Approve an actual-size proof for flyer and badge/ticket, a hardware-aware proof for the roll-up, and a scan test for every printed QR. Printer-ready source files are production precedents, not universal templates.

## Merchandise

Existing shirt, flag, sticker, and ticket artwork proves prior production, not a universal manufacturing standard. Start from approved vector art and obtain vendor specifications for ink, thread, material, minimum line, trapping, placement, safe area, and color matching. Approve a physical or photographed proof under realistic lighting before production. Do not alter the mark to accommodate a process; choose an approved variant or change the production method.
