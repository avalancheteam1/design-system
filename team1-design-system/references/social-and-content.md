# Social, editorial, and content

There is no current universal social template evidenced by the audited social folder; older GIFs and campaign graphics are precedents, not authority. Build from the global identity and current campaign brief, then verify the destination platform's latest dimensions and safe zones.

## Shared rules

- “Show, don't tell”: lead with one strong real image or one clear visual idea, not a paragraph.
- Use a short title, one verified supporting fact, and one action at most.
- Keep the Team1 host mark clear and current. Partner marks belong in an approved parity strip or lockup.
- Design for thumbnail recognition and alternate crops. Keep essential type and marks away from platform UI.
- Treat dates, metrics, applications, programs, handles, and links as time-sensitive.
- Do not use raw Drive media, member faces, stock/SFX, or campaign working files without explicit rights.

Common planning sizes include 1080 × 1350 portrait, 1080 × 1080 square, and 1080 × 1920 story, but they are starting points, not permanent platform law. Confirm the channel at production time and export each required ratio deliberately.

## Blog exception

The blog profile is intentionally distinct:

- Ava Blue `#3055B3` is the dominant background.
- Use a monochromatic image overlay and restrained network effects.
- Ava Red `#E6212F` is limited to the Team1 mark, icon, or key callout.
- Headlines may use Neuropol X or Inter in white. A subtle shadow or blur may protect legibility over detailed imagery.

This exception does not authorize Neuropol X/Inter or blue-dominant layouts as the global default. Verify font licensing before distribution.

## Copy and publishing

Write naturally for the audience and locale. Avoid corporate filler, unexplained crypto jargon, and generic AI phrasing. Claim only Team1's role; do not imply an Ava Labs, Avalanche Foundation, venue, sponsor, or partner endorsement without approval.

Before publishing, inspect the rendered post at actual feed size, check crops and compression, verify alt text/captions, links, tags, account, timing, and approvals, and retain the editable source plus final exports.
