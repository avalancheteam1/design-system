# Regional identity and localization

## Keep the core stable

Localize the message, imagery, language, cultural cues, city references, and approved partner area. Do not translate, redraw, or decorate the Team1 mark. Keep its clear space and hierarchy stable, and use only an approved chapter lockup.

## Chapter construction exception

Official chapter marks use a specific medium profile:

- the country's primary flag colors;
- Aeonik Black;
- an uppercase country label;
- a 3000 × 3000 px construction canvas;
- 344 px type;
- 788.5 px specified spacing;
- approved horizontal and square variants.

This is an explicit chapter-mark exception. It does not authorize all-caps `TEAM1`, flag colors in the global logo, Aeonik Black as general typography, or ad-hoc chapter marks. Use an approved source file; do not rebuild a missing one. Ambiguous or inconsistent country filenames require owner resolution before use.

## Localization contract

For each artifact, record:

- BCP 47 language and locale;
- embedded-text language, voiceover language, and caption/subtitle language;
- country/region name, approved spelling, date format, timezone, units, and reading direction;
- required script/font coverage and fallback;
- translator/reviewer and approval status.

Preserve Unicode in filenames, manifests, and exports. Test diacritics, non-Latin scripts, punctuation, text expansion, line breaks, right-to-left behavior where applicable, and font embedding. Do not infer a language from the country name or reuse a localized audio track with mismatched on-screen text.

## Regional events and decks

Use recognizable local places and genuine community activity without stereotyping. A local event concept may be culturally specific, but Team1 identity and approved endorsement relationships stay recognizable. Current regional decks can retain their inherited Inter and presentation furniture; those choices do not override global type or color.

Partner, venue, and government marks need explicit permission and an approved placement. Never copy handles, contacts, member photos, live QR destinations, or mutable schedules from a regional example. For a regional CTA, require an approved Team1/country route or tracking path supplied by its owner; never infer one from a country name or historical artifact. Do not translate protected names or URLs. Do not infer or claim chapter status from a folder, filename, old deck, or event example. Verify every local fact and publish date/time with an explicit timezone.
