# Team1 delivery QA

Run this checklist on the rendered final artifact, not only on source code or editable objects.

## Brief and factual integrity

- [ ] Deliverable, audience, language, dimensions, channel, deadline, and requested formats are confirmed.
- [ ] Dates, times, venue, speakers, partners, chapter status, metrics, links, handles, prices, and claims are verified and dated where needed.
- [ ] Missing facts remain explicit placeholders or are reported as blockers; none were invented.
- [ ] A QR exists only for an approved destination, scans from the final export, and is paired with a visible destination.

## Brand integrity

- [ ] Every mark comes from an approved source file and retains its aspect ratio, color, transparent padding, and visual clarity.
- [ ] No mark was redrawn, typeset, cropped from a screenshot, stretched, recolored, generated, rotated, or given new effects.
- [ ] Team1 remains the primary host identity; Avalanche and partners retain appropriate separation and hierarchy.
- [ ] Content signal, structural red, and theme accent roles have not been flattened into one arbitrary red.
- [ ] The authentic grid/glow background or a clearly documented derived translation is used.
- [ ] Photography is real, context-appropriate, permission-aware, and cropped without damaging faces or key action.
- [ ] No generic crypto cliché, fake interface, synthetic crowd, or invented chapter/program artwork appears.

## Typography and composition

- [ ] The artifact consciously uses overview/brand or editorial/case-study mode.
- [ ] Kanit, Inter, Inter SemiBold, and Helvetica Neue render as intended; no silent fallback is visible.
- [ ] New characters and the target language have correct glyph coverage, punctuation, accents, and line breaks.
- [ ] There is one dominant read, a clear support layer, and no unnecessary density.
- [ ] Titles, body, labels, images, marks, and actions align to a consistent grid and safe area.
- [ ] Copy was shortened before type was reduced; no overflow, clipping, collision, orphan, or accidental auto-shrink remains.
- [ ] Body copy stays in high-contrast areas away from strong glows and busy photography.

## Medium-specific checks

### Presentations

- [ ] Slide size, masters/layouts, backgrounds, frame, footer marks, and page numbering are intact.
- [ ] Every slide has been inspected individually and as a contact sheet.
- [ ] Slideshow mode has been tested in the target app.
- [ ] PDF page count, crop, fonts, hyperlinks, and playback fallback are correct.

### Social and raster exports

- [ ] Export dimensions and color profile match the destination.
- [ ] Essential content survives platform UI and likely alternate crops.
- [ ] Hierarchy works at thumbnail size and full size.
- [ ] Image quality is sufficient without oversharpening or compression artifacts.

### Print and PDF

- [ ] Page size, bleed, trim, live area, image resolution, and printer requirements are correct.
- [ ] Body text is readable in a printed proof and grayscale hierarchy remains clear.
- [ ] Fonts are embedded or converted according to the printer’s requirements.

### Web and documents

- [ ] Text remains real/selectable where appropriate; headings and reading order are semantic.
- [ ] Contrast, alt text, keyboard access, focus state, responsive crop, and link destination are checked.

## Delivery

- [ ] Editable source and requested export are both present and open successfully.
- [ ] Filenames are clear, versioned when needed, and free of “final-final” ambiguity.
- [ ] No maintainer-specific absolute path, secret, private identifier, hidden placeholder, stale note, or unapproved source file is embedded.
- [ ] Any substitution, derived rule, missing asset, or unresolved approval is disclosed in the handoff.
